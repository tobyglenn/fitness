How This Works
The What-If Schedule Lab uses your historical training data to model how different workout intensities impact your recovery. Based on 8 days of WHOOP recovery data and 117 recorded workouts, I've built a personalized fatigue model. Drag workouts between days to see real-time projections of your recovery curve throughout the week. The model accounts for both the immediate strain impact and the natural recovery trend over 1-3 days.
