How I Calculate This

Recovery Score comes from WHOOP's daily recovery reading (0–100). It factors in HRV, resting heart rate, sleep performance, and recent strain.


Recommendation logic:

  • recovery ≥ 75% + load not increasing → 🔥 Intense
  • recovery ≥ 75% + load increasing → ✅ Normal (don't pile on)
  • recovery 50–74% → ✅ Normal
  • recovery 33–49% → 🧘 Light
  • recovery < 33% → 💤 Rest

Training load trend: compares last 3 days of Tonal volume vs the 3 days before that. If recent > prior × 1.1, load is considered increasing.


7-day forecast: Today uses actual WHOOP data. Future days simulate recovery impact — intense sessions drain ~15 pts, rest days restore ~15 pts — to help visualize training arc.


This is a heuristic model, not medical advice. Trust your body.
