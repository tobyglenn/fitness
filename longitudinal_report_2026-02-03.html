Longitudinal Performance Report

Analysis as of 2026-02-03

This report provides a long-term perspective on your training load and recovery markers, helping you understand how different training phases impact your physiological readiness over time. This is critical for progressive overload and avoiding overtraining as a "Technical Athlete".

1. Longitudinal Training Load Trend

Insight: This chart tracks your cumulative training stress over a 28-day rolling window, incorporating both lifting volume and estimated running load. Observe the peaks and valleys to correlate with your training blocks. Sustained increases in load should ideally be matched by robust recovery metrics to ensure adaptation, not just accumulation of fatigue.

2. Longitudinal Recovery Markers Trend

Insight: Key physiological recovery markers—Whoop Recovery, Unified HRV, Sleep Performance, and Garmin Body Battery—are shown as 7-day rolling averages. Healthy recovery trends are indicated by higher Recovery/HRV/BB scores and consistent sleep performance. Significant dips often signal accumulated fatigue or external stressors, requiring adjustments to training or lifestyle.

3. Training Load vs. Overall Readiness Score

Insight: This integrated view highlights the interplay between your training load and a composite "Overall Readiness Score". Ideally, periods of increasing training load should be met with maintained or slightly decreasing readiness scores (indicating adaptation). A sharp decline in readiness alongside high load might suggest overreaching, while consistently low readiness with moderate load could indicate poor recovery strategies.

Data compiled from Whoop, Garmin, Eight Sleep, Speediance. Running load multiplier is an estimation and may require calibration.
