📖 How to Read This

Readiness %
If you trained less than your optimal rest window ago, readiness = (hours elapsed / optimal hours) × 100%. Once past optimal, small performance loss accrues.
Optimal Rest
The rest duration that historically correlates with your highest volume sessions for that muscle group. Your personal data, not generic guidelines.
Depreciation Rate
Estimated % performance drop per extra day beyond optimal rest. Low rate = muscle maintains readiness long after optimal window.
