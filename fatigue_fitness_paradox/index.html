🎯 Training Guidance

  • Keep your A:C Ratio between 0.8 and 1.3 — this is the sweet spot for maximizing fitness gains while minimizing injury risk.
  • When ratio climbs above 1.3, reduce training intensity (not volume) first — swap hard sessions for moderate ones.
  • If ratio exceeds 1.5 for 3+ days, take a structured deload week: 40-60% of normal volume and intensity.
  • Don't confuse fatigue with overtraining — some fatigue is normal and required for adaptation. The ratio tells you if it's too much.
  • Build a deload week every 4th week proactively — don't wait for the ratio to spike.
  • WHOOP recovery score below 33% consistently + high ratio = stop and rest. Recovery score above 67% = safe to push.
